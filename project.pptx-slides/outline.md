# DCOMP320 — 26-Slide Presentation & Viva Defense Outline

## Overview & Purpose
A comprehensive, beginner-friendly and mathematically rigorous English presentation deck designed for a team of students presenting to Lecturer Amandus Benjamin Coker and examination panels at Limkokwing University of Creative Technology, Sierra Leone.

## Key Upgrades & Features
- **Morph Transitions & Visual Glides**: Applied across sequential states (Tower of Hanoi step-by-step moves, Huffman tree building, graph path highlights).
- **Team Presentation Blueprint**: Clear presenter badges (`Presenter 1` through `Presenter 4`), timing guides, and speaking scripts.
- **Dedicated Viva Defense Section**: 5 dedicated slides addressing the most difficult examiner viva questions (Figure 2 anomaly, Dijkstra vs Prim proof, Level-order FIFO vs DFS LIFO, Huffman greedy proof, Hanoi induction proof).
- **Cross-Verification**: 19 automated unit tests, Floyd-Warshall and Kruskal independent validations.
